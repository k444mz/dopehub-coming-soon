Privacy notice

This notice covers sign-ups for updates and early access, and team applications, on this page. Last updated 1 October 2026.

Who we are

DopeHub.net, based in the United Kingdom. Contact us at contact@dopehub.net.

What we collect

  • Updates and early access: your email address, separate newsletter and beta preferences, and the dates you requested and confirmed them. Existing optional survey answers are retained.
  • Team applications: your name, email, chosen role, availability, optional link and your message.

Visitor statistics: we count page visits without cookies. We record the page, the website you came from, and your screen size group (mobile, tablet or desktop). To count unique visitors, we use a scrambled code made from your IP address and browser that changes every day and is never stored. We never store your IP address. If your browser sends a "Do Not Track" or "Global Privacy Control" signal, we don't count you at all.

We don't use tracking cookies or advertising pixels.

Why and on what basis

We use your details for the preferences you confirm: occasional newsletters and launch news, beta access messages, or reviewing and replying to your team application. Requesting beta access does not subscribe you to the newsletter. Our lawful basis is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

Where it's kept and who sees it

On our own server in the UK. Automated emails are sent through Oracle Email Delivery; human correspondence uses our email host. We don't sell your data or share it with marketing companies.

How long we keep it

Your email until you withdraw the relevant preferences or ask us to delete it. Newsletters have an unsubscribe link; beta messages have a separate cancellation link. Older unsubscribe links still remove the original combined signup. Applications for up to 12 months, unless you join the team.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or withdraw consent, by emailing contact@dopehub.net. If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Stop these emails?

This link stops the messages it relates to. A newsletter unsubscribe keeps your beta request; a beta cancellation keeps your newsletter preference. Older combined-signup links remove both. You can also contact us to delete your details.
